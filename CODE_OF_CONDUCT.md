# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technical preference.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports a healthy community include:

- demonstrating empathy, patience, and respect;
- giving specific, evidence-based technical feedback;
- accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- acknowledging model limitations and uncertainty; and
- prioritizing the safety and privacy of contributors and data subjects.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, sustained disruption, and knowingly submitting confidential or re-identifiable facility data.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that conflict with this code. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers involved in a report must recuse themselves from its handling.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, and adapted for this project's size and data-safety requirements.
